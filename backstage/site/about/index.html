About us

This isn't what social media was meant to be.So we built it again.

A social network you can put down. Built only from people you've actually met.

Why we exist

We built the thing we couldn't find.

A network where the feed has an end. Where your photos have a number. Where nobody sells your attention while you look.

We're not against phones. We use them. We're against what they were turned into. And we know we're part of the same system. That's why it had to be built, not just said.

Our story

  1. 01/ 03

    The pause

    After the lockdowns, most of our friendships lived on a screen. Then the doors opened. The apps didn't let go. We went back out and kept scrolling, right past the people in front of us.

How it works

Four steps. No search.

Every connection starts offline. There is no search. No usernames to find, no suggestions of people you might know. You connect one way: someone you meet scans your code. Everyone has one. It's on your phone, and on the back of every piece in this shop. The shirt is the gateway: you link the code once after it arrives, and from then on it points to you.

    Wear it.

04

Concept

Social media, with the parts that hurt taken out.

Twelve screens from the app. And what's behind each one.

01 / 12

A feed that ends

Twelve stories a day, then it's over. Posts disappear after 45 days; what should stay goes into your memory vault. Strangers' profiles stay invisible, even when friends share their content.

Live pulse

What's happening near you, right now. Share where you are and friends come find you. Only the ones you know.

A map of real places

Verified cafés, bars and shows that joined the network. Nobody can buy a better spot.

A profile with twelve photos

No highlight reel, no counter. Twelve pictures, what you're into, what you're planning. Nobody you haven't met sees your profile.

What you're about

What you're into and what you're up to next. Visible only to the people you've met.

Messages that stay yours

Private by default, encrypted by design, screenshot-protected. No AI reads along. Notifications are off until you turn them on.

Direct messages

Only with people whose code you've scanned. No requests from strangers.

Group chats with an expiry

For the concert, the trip, the weekend. Nobody gets added unasked. When it's over, the chat is over.

Real conversations

No AI in between. Just the two of you.

Communities

People into the same thing. Join, show up.

Channels

Follow brands and makers without an algorithm deciding what you see.

Calendar

Your plans and events nearby, at a glance.

Where it's going

Small company. Big mission.

Bring people together where they actually are. At home, in your city, and everywhere you travel.
