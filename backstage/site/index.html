07

Questions

Got questions? Here are answers.

Who or what is txtme.social?

A European social media app, built in Amsterdam. It starts where the others stop: in real life. You're only connected to people you've actually met. No algorithm, no ads, no data selling.

Does txtme.social have any algorithms?

No recommendation algorithm. Your feed shows what your connections share, in the order it happened. Twelve stories a day, then it ends. Nobody decides for you what you should see.

What is the txtme.social merch?

T-shirts, tank tops, cropped tops, plus caps and bags. Every piece carries a QR code that leads to your personal profile in the app. Someone you meet scans it, and you're connected. The shirt is the gateway, not the product.

How can I pay for my merch?

Through Mollie in Amsterdam: Wero, iDEAL, credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, Bancomat Pay or direct bank transfer. You enter your payment details at Mollie, not with us. All prices include VAT.
