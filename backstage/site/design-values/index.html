Design values

The system behind txtme.social.

Every colour, size and movement in the preview comes from this sheet. Colours, type samples and buttons below are the live elements of the shop, not pictures of them.

01

Colour

Black carries the page. Cyan and pink are the brand and appear as a signal — in lines, numbers, buttons and headlines — never as decoration. Each page has one light section in bone.

Base

  • Ink#0B0B0CPage background
  • Surface#131315Image fields, tiles
  • Surface 2#1A1A1DSecond surface
  • Bone#EFECE6The light section on each page
  • Bone 2#E5E1D9Second light surface

Text

  • Bright#F4F2EEText on dark — off-white, never pure white
  • Muted#9D9B96Body copy, secondary information
  • Faint#66645FLabels, inactive states

Brand

  • Cyan#60C4E3Brand colour — signal and gradient
  • Pink#EB5986Brand colour — signal and gradient

Gradients

  • Brand#60C4E3 → #EB5986Lines, accents, active states
  • Soft#9FDCF0 → #CFD5FF → #F2A7BFHeadlines and main buttons on dark
  • Soft on bone#2A9CC2 → #6B6FD8 → #D9467AThe same gradient on the light section

03

Space

Generous and consistent. The same few measures repeat on every page, so the shop reads as one piece.

Page width
1480 px maximum, centred
Side margin
20 px on phones · 32 px from 640 px
Section spacing
96 px · 144 px from 640 px · 176 px from 1024 px (top and bottom)
Compact sections
64 px · 96 px from 640 px
Breakpoints
640 px · 1024 px · 1280 px
Touch targets
at least 44 × 44 px
Product images
4 : 5, the space is reserved before the image loads — nothing jumps

05

Motion

Calm and precise. Movement guides the eye; it never gets in the way of reading or scrolling.

One easing for the whole site
cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)
What moves
only position and opacity — nothing that changes the layout
Scrolling
native, never taken over by the page
Reduced motion
if the device asks for it, animations are switched off
Benchmark
smooth on a phone with a six-times slower processor, not on the studio Mac
