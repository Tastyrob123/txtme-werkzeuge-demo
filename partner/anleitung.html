Partner Pack · guide

One recipient, one page.

Partner Pack makes the personal proposal for a partner and the personal invitation for a creator: as a web page and as a PDF. The look of both comes from the finished templates and never changes here. You write what is true for this one recipient.

1
New recipient

«New recipient», choose Partner or Ambassador, write the name. For an ambassador choose the kind of creator (travel, YouTube, niche, NGO, local): the invitation gets the page for that kind. A partner starts from the general version of the proposal with all texts filled in; an ambassador starts from the template with placeholders in brackets.

2
Fill in the fields

The fields stand in the order of the page. Write only what is true and was agreed: no prices, no user numbers, nothing that someone outside txtme.social would have to deliver. In headlines the quiet, lighter part stands between two underscores: Nicht sponsern. _Festhalten._ Photos come from the photos of the template.

3
Save

Changed fields make the Save button yellow. Save (or Cmd/Ctrl + S) puts them into the draft. The receipt offers «Undo». Nothing is saved silently, and the browser asks before you leave with unsaved fields.

4
Look at the draft

«Draft as a page» builds the page from the saved fields and opens it. «Before, after» shows every changed field and the published page next to the draft.

5
Publish

Publishing builds the page on a test copy first, checks it and only then replaces the published page. If anything fails, the published page stays as it was. Required fields that are empty or still a placeholder hold publishing back; the message names them.

6
PDF

«Make PDF» prints the published page for print (A4) with Google Chrome on this computer. After the next publication make it again, so the PDF matches the page.

7
Earlier versions

Every publication is kept. «Earlier versions» puts an older one back into the draft, never straight onto the page: you check and publish again. «First version» brings back the fields as they were when the recipient was created.

8
Remove

«Remove this recipient» asks for the name. Nothing is deleted: the recipient and its page go into the archive folder of the tool.

Demo

Everything runs on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is sent to the recipient: you send the link or the PDF yourself. The invitation of Jonas Verhoeven is the invented example of the template.
