Open the Brand Book

01Overview

How the Brand Book isbuilt.

The Brand Book is the brand of txtme.social to work with: four parts for daily work and the brand guidelines to look things up.

  1. 1On the left you find the parts: Photo archive, Logo & files and Brand check under «Work», Voice & lines and the Brand guidelines under «Reference».
  2. 2At the top right you choose the language: English or German. It stays chosen the next time you open the Brand Book.
  3. 3At the bottom there is always the note on image rights. Next to it «All files (ZIP)» downloads every file of the brand at once.
Brand Book

02Photo archive

Find aphoto.

Every photo of the brand is in the photo archive, each with its area, format and source.

  1. 1Type into the search field, for example «tram» or «tee».
  2. 2Narrow it down: area (shoot, merch, app, hero, Instagram, QR code), portrait or landscape, and source.
  3. 3Above the photos it says how many match. «Reset filters» shows all of them again.
Brand Book

03Photo archive

Download in the rightsize.

A click on a photo opens it large. On the right there are four ready-made sizes.

  1. 1Point at «Instagram post» (1080 × 1350) or «Story» (1080 × 1920). The crop appears in the picture.
  2. 2Drag the frame to the part that should be seen. «Download» takes exactly this crop.
  3. 3«Website» gives the photo with 2000 pixels on the longest side, «Print» the largest version there is.
Brand Book

04Photo archive

What was created withAI.

Three figures were generated from studio photos of txtme.social. They are marked everywhere.

  1. 1In the archive they carry the sign «AI». Under «Source» you show them alone or hide them.
  2. 2When you open one, a dark note above the details says: use it only with this note.
  3. 3When you download one, the file name contains «ai-generated».
Brand Book

05Logo & files

Logo and files for anagency.

«Logo & files» has everything an agency, a shop or a printer needs.

  1. 1The logo in three versions as SVG and PNG. SVG stays sharp at any size, PNG has a transparent background.
  2. 2Below it you find the pixel versions as they are used in the shop, and the typeface with its licence.
  3. 3«All files (ZIP)» at the top downloads everything at once.
Brand Book

06Logo & files

Copy a colourvalue.

Each colour is shown with its name and role. One click is enough.

  1. 1Click a colour: its value, for example #0B0B0C, is on the clipboard.
  2. 2A click on the RGB line copies the RGB values.
  3. 3Below them you download the colours as a file: ASE for design programs, CSS for the website, JSON for developers.
Brand Book

07Voice & lines

Write in the voice of thebrand.

Here is the Brand Voice of txtme.social, word for word, and below it the lines that are already in use.

  1. 1Read the voice from top to bottom: what it is, the tone, the topics, the style.
  2. 2Each line in use says where it appears. «Copy» puts it on the clipboard exactly as it is.
  3. 3«The whole voice in the guidelines» opens the chapter on the voice.
Brand Book

08Brand check

Check adraft.

When a partner sends a poster, a flyer or a post, you check here whether it fits txtme.

  1. 1Drag the draft into the field or «Choose file». An image (JPG, PNG, WebP) or a PDF, up to 30 MB.
  2. 2Press «Check». The colours are measured against the palette of the brand.
  3. 3Below the empty field you always see an example, clearly marked as such.
Brand Book

09Brand check

Read thefeedback.

On the left you see the measured colours: how much of the surface is in the colours of the brand, and which tones are foreign.

  1. 1Each tone has a verdict: same, close or foreign, with the nearest colour of the brand.
  2. 2Only the colours are measured. The note «Probe without AI» says so; type, logo, image and tone are not assessed.
  3. 3«Copy as text» gives you the feedback for a message. «Save result» puts it as a file on your computer, only if you want it.
Brand Book

10Brand guidelines

Look it up in theguidelines.

The brand guidelines have ten chapters: voice, logo, colours and where they come from, typeface, space, images, motion, fixed lines and files.

  1. 1Press «Brand guidelines» at the top right or in the list on the left.
  2. 2The bar at the top takes you to each chapter. The logo at the top left brings you back to the Brand Book.
  3. 3«Save as PDF» makes a PDF of all the guidelines to pass on.
