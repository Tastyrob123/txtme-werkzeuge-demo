Glossary (see · reason · do)

HERKUNFT
See: «Request from another site refused.» · Reason: the request did not come from this page. · Do: open the workshop from its own address and try again.
FORM
See: «Only JSON.» · Reason: a request without JSON body. · Do: reload the workshop.
GROESSE
See: «Request too large.» · Reason: more than 60 KB were sent. · Do: shorten the description.
GRENZE
See: «Too many drafts in a short time.» · Reason: more than 40 drafts per hour. · Do: wait an hour.
KI-DIENST
See: «The draft did not work.» · Reason: the AI service answered with an error or is switched off (demo). · Do: use the templates; the AI is switched on only with DRUCK_KI=1 and a key from the environment.
STORAGE
See: «The browser storage is full.» · Reason: localStorage holds about 5 MB. · Do: save pieces as a working file, then delete old ones.
FONT
See: a PDF without Space Grotesk. · Reason: a font file in haeuser/txtme/schriften is missing. · Do: run bau.py txtme again.
