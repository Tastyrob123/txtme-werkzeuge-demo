How Chat Team works.

Chat Team is the page behind the chat on txtme.social. The chat answers customers from the wording of the website and the catalogue; this is where the team sees what comes out of it and what it has to do. Nothing is sent from here, and nothing is published.

01

Inbox

Every request from the site and the chat window: waiting list, press, partners, support, messages. A new request is marked «New»; opening it marks it as read.

  1. Open a request on the left. On the right you see the details, the message and, if the customer wrote in the chat, the conversation.
  2. «Write the letter» drafts a reply. Work in it like in Canva: click selects a part, drag moves it, the corners change the size, double click writes. «Text only» shows subject and text as plain fields.
  3. «Copy text» puts the letter on the clipboard, «Save as PDF» prints it as it stands. Paste it into your mail program and send it yourself.
  4. «Done» ticks the request off, «Open again» brings it back. The bin deletes it for good, for example when the customer asks for it.

Requests in another language carry «Read in English»: a reading copy that is not sent.

02

What was asked

Every question to the chat, by topic, with the answer the chat gave. Questions marked «without answer» had nothing on the website. «Add the answer» writes it once; from then on the chat answers with it. While the online part is off (see «Check»), that is the chat of this tool; the chat on the website gets it once the online part is switched on. «Tick off without answer» keeps the question in the log without the mark.

03

Added by the team

The answers you added. Change or remove them here when something changes. The entries marked «Example» are invented and the chat does not know them.

04

What the chat knows

The memory of the chat by area: the company, the app, the pieces, shipping and payment, press, contact, events. Tap a row to change it or to switch it off; «As on the website» brings the original back. «New knowledge» takes written text straight in; links, documents and photos need the model.

«Check the chat» asks the chat with the knowledge as it stands now, without the question going into the log.

05

Try the chat

The button at the top right opens the chat the way customers see it. Questions from there count as examples.

06

Demo and the real chat

In this demo the chat answers without a model, from the recorded answers and the knowledge; the drafts quote no facts. The real chat on txtme.social keeps its questions and requests online; the connection is switched on once at delivery and needs the key of the online part. The check page shows the state of everything.
